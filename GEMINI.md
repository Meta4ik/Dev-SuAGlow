# SuA Glow Development & Visual Guidelines

## Visual & Image Generation Standards
- **Authentic Clinic Backgrounds**: For all future renderings, backgrounds, and campaign composites, always use authentic SuA Glow clinic photos from the official Google Places / Google Business profile as the visual reference (`ImagePaths`).
- **Zone Differentiation**:
  - **Lobby & Reception**: Vertical light wood-slat feature wall, dimensional "SuA K-Glow" wall logo lettering, modern white reception desk, and consultation areas. Use for greeting shots, brand hero portraits, and consultations.
  - **Private Treatment Suites / Procedure Rooms**: Ergonomic white leather clinical treatment recliners/beds, soft indirect cove lighting, minimalist warm taupe and off-white walls with subtle wood accents. Use for active clinical procedures (needle-free DEP follicle infusion, LDM, SkinPen, facials). **Never place treatment beds or clinical procedures in the reception lobby.**
  - **Treatment Room Background Rule**: Keep the treatment room background clean, peaceful, and uncluttered. **Do not show device consoles, machines, or equipment carts in the background.** Focus purely on the aesthetician, handheld treatment applicator, and patient's hair/scalp.
- **Signature Aesthetics**:
  - Warm neutral tones, minimalist medical aesthetic, warm taupe/champagne accents (`#AA987C`, `#E5B869`).
  - Realistic Korean clinical aesthetics, needle-free DEP treatment devices, and modern consultation spaces.
