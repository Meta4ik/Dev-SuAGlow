WHAT TO EXPECT AFTER TREATMENT

IMMEDIATELY AFTER TREATMENT

Mild redness, temporary warmth, slight swelling, or skin sensitivity. Most patients return to normal activities shortly afterward.

1–4 WEEKS

Skin may gradually begin appearing firmer, smoother, tighter, and more refreshed.

2–3 MONTHS

Collagen remodeling continues over time as skin quality and contour refinement gradually develop.

MAINTENANCE

Many patients pursue maintenance treatments depending on age, collagen quality, lifestyle, skin goals, and preventative aging strategy.

CANDIDATE & CARE DETAILS

IDEAL CANDIDATES

Great for improving mild skin laxity, early facial sagging, jawline definition, skin firmness, fine lines, collagen support, preventative aging concerns, and overall skin quality. Popular among patients in their late 20s (prevention), 30s (maintenance), and 40s+ (contour and skin support).

WHO MAY NOT BE SUITABLE

Pregnancy, implanted electronic devices, active skin infections, recent procedures, or certain medical conditions.

COMBINATION TREATMENTS

Often combined with skin hydration, Korean skin boosters, LDM ultrasound facials, SkinTox, and glass skin protocols.
