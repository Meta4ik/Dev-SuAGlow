Configured strictly to "Presence: People in or regularly in your included locations". Unlike Google's default "Presence or Interest" setting, this eliminates clicks from non-residents browsing Texas while away.

The SuA Glow clinic is conveniently situated on State Highway 121 (Sam Rayburn Tollway) in Carrollton, enabling effortless 10–20 minute transit from Plano, Frisco, Lewisville, The Colony, and Flower Mound.

3. High-Income Demographic Alignment

The 13-city cluster encompasses ~3.8 million residents with household incomes well above state averages ($115k+ median in Frisco/Plano/Flower Mound), perfectly matching elective $399–$2,400 clinical hair treatments.
