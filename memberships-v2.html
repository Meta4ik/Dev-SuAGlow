Membership FAQs

Everything you need to know about our monthly five-layer ritual, membership pricing, and VIP benefits.

1. What is the difference between Glass Skin Essential ($199) and Glass Skin Method ($299)?

SuA Glow offers two distinct monthly membership tiers designed around Korean skincare philosophy:

• Glass Skin Essential ($199/mo): A 60-minute foundational facial ritual utilizing our EVE-Omega platform. Features a Korean double cleanse, gentle dermabrasion, AquaFacial pore refining, dual-prong microcurrent/EMS facial sculpting, a custom Korean hydrating molding mask, and medical LED red light therapy.

• Glass Skin Method™ ($299/mo): Our all-inclusive clinical five-layer ritual combining medical AquaFacial, needle-free DEP skin booster infusion, LDM water drop ultrasound, Celluma LED phototherapy, and a Glutathione IV push (administered when clinically appropriate), plus the monthly rotating Seoul Drop.

2. Can I switch between the Essential and Method memberships?

Yes, absolutely! You can upgrade, downgrade, or switch between Glass Skin Essential ($199) and Glass Skin Method ($299) at any time. Simply notify our front desk before your next monthly billing date, and your membership will adjust seamlessly.

3. Who is the Korean Glass Skin Membership for?

The membership is designed for adults who want a consistent, skin-first approach to hydration, radiance, texture, and overall skin quality.

Your provider evaluates your skin, goals, medical history, and treatment candidacy to determine whether the ritual—and each included layer—is appropriate for you.

4. Is the Glass Skin Ritual personalized for my skin?

Yes. In both memberships, our licensed providers customize the active serums and targeted steps around your current skin needs. For Method members, your Skin Booster Edit is tailored to focus on DEWY, TONE-UP, CALM, or SMOOTH based on clinical evaluation.

5. How often should I get my membership treatment?

Both memberships are designed around one complete ritual each month.

The Korean philosophy is built on cumulative consistency rather than harsh, irregular procedures. One monthly session maintains cellular turnover, barrier hydration, and luminous skin quality all year round.

6. Are there any contracts or cancellation fees?

No. Both memberships are month-to-month with no long-term contracts or enrollment fees. You may pause or cancel at any time with simple written notice prior to your next renewal.

7. Is there any downtime or discomfort with the 5-layer ritual?

The facial and device-based layers are designed to be gentle, with little to no expected downtime for most appropriate candidates.

The Glutathione IV Push involves IV administration and is included only when medically appropriate. Individual experiences, treatment recommendations, and responses vary.

8. Can I pause or cancel my membership?

Yes. The Glass Skin Membership is designed without a long-term membership commitment.

Pauses, cancellations, and other membership changes are subject to the notice requirements and terms outlined in your membership agreement.

9. Where is the Korean Glass Skin Membership available?

The Korean Glass Skin Membership is available exclusively at SuA Glow Medical Aesthetics in Carrollton, Texas, serving Dallas–Fort Worth and surrounding communities including Plano, Frisco, Coppell, Addison, Grapevine, and Southlake.

10. What is the Seoul Drop?

The Seoul Drop is your rotating K-Beauty discovery included with the Glass Skin Membership.

Selections may include curated skincare extras, masks, ampoules, or seasonal finishing touches, with the featured discovery changing periodically.
