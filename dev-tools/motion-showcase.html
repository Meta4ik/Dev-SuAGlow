Zero-Jitter Normalized Momentum

Standard browser scroll jumps in discrete 100px increments. Lenis converts mouse wheel and trackpad impulses into smooth cubic or exponential curves.

Result: Silky 120Hz refresh on ProMotion displays

GSAP ScrollTrigger Sync

By piping lenis.on('scroll', ScrollTrigger.update) and driving Lenis via gsap.ticker, visual scrub timelines never jitter out of phase.

Result: Zero tearing on pinned or parallax sections

Non-Destructive Scroll Locking

Opening a consultation booking modal or navigation drawer? lenis.stop() freezes scroll without resetting the page position or breaking layout flow.
