SuA Glow: Master Content Section Compendium

This guide outlines the different types of content sections available across the SuA Glow website.

Use this compendium as a visual menu of options when planning content for new pages. Providing content that fits these structures helps maintain design consistency.

1. Hero & Introductions

The first impression of a page, typically containing a large headline, background image/video, and a call-to-action.

Standard Image Hero (Centered)

Centered text overlaying a background image.

Pages: about, cosmetic-tox, microneedling, radiesse, seoul-man, skin-health, ldm-water-drop-lifting, oligio-x

Standard Image Hero (Left-Aligned)

Left-aligned text overlaying a background image.

Pages: dep, medical-weight-loss-dallas, needleless-infusion-therapy

Split Layout Hero

Half text, half image layout.

Pages: shop-skincare

Solid/Gradient Hero

Minimalist hero with no background image.

Pages: bridal-glow, dermal-filler, injectables, medical-tox, one-day-glow, salmon-pn-facial, scar-treatment, signature-treatment, skin-quality-boosters

2. Treatment Explanations ("What Is It?")

Sections designed to explain exactly what a treatment is, how it works, and the core technology behind it.

Standard Explanation (what-is)

Text beside an explanatory image.

Pages: bridal-glow, cosmetic-tox, dermal-filler, microneedling, radiesse, salmon-pn-facial, signature-treatment, seoul-man

Treatment Areas

Visual breakdown of targetable areas.

Pages: cosmetic-tox, medical-tox

Benefits Grid

Icon-driven grid highlighting key perks.

Pages: bridal-glow, dermal-filler, radiesse, salmon-pn-facial, signature-treatment, one-day-glow

Expectations / Timeline (expect)

5. Guides & FAQs

FAQ Accordion

Collapsible list of questions and answers.

Pages: bridal-glow, dep, oligio-x, radiesse, medical-weight-loss-dallas, seoul-man, signature-treatment, skin-quality-boosters

Standard Flow Blueprint

When writing copy for a new treatment page, follow this flow:

  1. hero (Hook & Intro)
  2. what-is (The Explanation)
  3. treatment-benefits (The Value)
  4. expect (The Experience)
  5. why-sua (The Differentiator)
  6. faq (The Reassurance)
