# SuA Glow: Master Content Section Compendium

This guide outlines the different types of content sections available across the SuA Glow website and lists the specific pages where each section type is currently used. 

Use this compendium as a menu of options when planning content for new pages or updating existing ones. By providing content that fits these structures, we can maintain consistency across the entire site.

## 1. Hero & Introductions
The first impression of a page, typically containing a large headline, background image/video, and a call-to-action. There are several structural variations of heroes used:

* **Video Background Hero**: Full-height (`min-h-screen`) hero featuring an autoplaying background video. (*[index](../index.html)*)
* **Standard Image Hero (Centered)**: Centered text overlaying a background image. (*[about](../about.html), [cosmetic-tox](../cosmetic-tox.html), [ldm-water-drop-lifting](../ldm-water-drop-lifting.html), [microneedling](../microneedling.html), [oligio-x](../oligio-x.html), [radiesse](../radiesse.html), [seoul-man](../seoul-man.html), [skin-health](../skin-health.html)*)
* **Standard Image Hero (Left-Aligned)**: Left-aligned text overlaying a background image. (*[dep](../dep.html), [medical-weight-loss-dallas](../medical-weight-loss-dallas.html), [needleless-infusion-therapy](../needleless-infusion-therapy.html)*)
* **Solid/Gradient Background Hero**: Minimalist hero with no background image, just clean text on a solid or gradient background. (*[bridal-glow](../bridal-glow.html), [collagen-stimulation-facial](../collagen-stimulation-facial.html), [contact](../contact.html), [dermal-filler](../dermal-filler.html), [injectables](../injectables.html), [medical-tox](../medical-tox.html), [one-day-glow](../one-day-glow.html), [referral](../referral.html), [salmon-pn-facial](../salmon-pn-facial.html), [scar-treatment](../scar-treatment.html), [signature-treatment](../signature-treatment.html), [skin-quality-boosters](../skin-quality-boosters.html), [testimonials](../testimonials.html), [tone-up-facial](../tone-up-facial.html)*)
* **Split Layout Hero**: Half text, half image layout. (*[shop-skincare](../shop-skincare.html)*)
* **Utility `page-hero`**: A simplified, shorter hero used exclusively for utility and legal pages (*[accessibility-statement](../accessibility-statement.html), [coming-soon](../coming-soon.html), [cookie-notices-choices](../cookie-notices-choices.html), [financing](../financing.html), [hippa-policy](../hippa-policy.html), [privacy-policy](../privacy-policy.html), [shop-skincare-rejuran](../shop-skincare-rejuran.html)*)
* **`showcase-banner`**: A highly visual product/treatment banner that acts as a secondary hero (*[oligio-x](../oligio-x.html)*)

## 2. Treatment Explanations & Overviews ("What Is It?")
Sections designed to explain exactly what a treatment is, how it works, and the core technology behind it.
* **`what-is` / `what-are`**: The standard explanation block (*[bridal-glow](../bridal-glow.html), [cosmetic-tox](../cosmetic-tox.html), [dermal-filler](../dermal-filler.html), [microneedling](../microneedling.html), [radiesse](../radiesse.html), [salmon-pn-facial](../salmon-pn-facial.html), [signature-treatment](../signature-treatment.html), etc.*)
* **`how-it-works`**: Technical breakdown of the process (*[dep](../dep.html), [needleless-infusion-therapy](../needleless-infusion-therapy.html)*)
* **`treatment-modes` / `treatment-zones`**: Specific details on how a machine or treatment targets the skin (*[ldm-water-drop-lifting](../ldm-water-drop-lifting.html), [oligio-x](../oligio-x.html)*)
* **`treatment-areas`**: Lists the specific areas of the body/face treated (*[cosmetic-tox](../cosmetic-tox.html), [medical-tox](../medical-tox.html)*)
* **`scar-types`**: Specific breakdown of conditions treated (*[scar-treatment](../scar-treatment.html)*)

## 3. Benefits, Expectations & Value Propositions
Sections focused on why the patient should choose this treatment and what they can expect from it.
* **`treatment-benefits`**: Bulleted or grid highlights of the treatment's upside (*[bridal-glow](../bridal-glow.html), [dermal-filler](../dermal-filler.html), [radiesse](../radiesse.html), [salmon-pn-facial](../salmon-pn-facial.html), [signature-treatment](../signature-treatment.html)*)
* **`expect`**: What the patient will experience during or after the treatment (*[bridal-glow](../bridal-glow.html), [cosmetic-tox](../cosmetic-tox.html), [dermal-filler](../dermal-filler.html), [microneedling](../microneedling.html), [radiesse](../radiesse.html), [seoul-man](../seoul-man.html), etc.*)
* **`why-sua` / `why-choose-us`**: Why the patient should choose SuA Glow over a competitor for this specific treatment (*[bridal-glow](../bridal-glow.html), [cosmetic-tox](../cosmetic-tox.html), [dermal-filler](../dermal-filler.html), [radiesse](../radiesse.html), [signature-treatment](../signature-treatment.html), [medical-weight-loss-dallas](../medical-weight-loss-dallas.html)*)
* **`why-skinpen` / `why-we-chose` / `why-seoul-clinics`**: Justifying the specific technology chosen (*[microneedling](../microneedling.html), [oligio-x](../oligio-x.html), [ldm-water-drop-lifting](../ldm-water-drop-lifting.html)*)

## 4. Social Proof & Results
Sections that build trust through real patient results and testimonials.
* **`before-after-gallery`**: Visual grid of patient results (*[radiesse](../radiesse.html)*)
* **`glow-stories`**: Patient testimonials and reviews (*[index](../index.html)*)
* **`clinical-cases`**: Medical or scientific evidence of results (*[oligio-x](../oligio-x.html)*)

## 5. Guides, FAQs & Next Steps
Actionable information for the patient.
* **`faq`**: Frequently asked questions accordion (*used across almost 20+ treatment pages including [bridal-glow](../bridal-glow.html), [dep](../dep.html), [oligio-x](../oligio-x.html), [radiesse](../radiesse.html), etc.*)
* **`post-treatment-guide` / `treatment-guide`**: Aftercare and maintenance instructions (*[oligio-x](../oligio-x.html), [ldm-water-drop-lifting](../ldm-water-drop-lifting.html)*)
* **`schedule` / `consultation`**: Call-to-action blocks to book an appointment (*[medical-weight-loss-dallas](../medical-weight-loss-dallas.html), [skin-quality-boosters](../skin-quality-boosters.html)*)
* **`financing-widget`**: Information on payment plans (*[financing](../financing.html)*)

## 6. Philosophy & Brand Approach
Sections detailing the unique K-Beauty methodology and SuA Glow's ethos.
* **`philosophy` / `philosophies`**: The overarching approach to a treatment (*[bridal-glow](../bridal-glow.html), [collagen-stimulation-facial](../collagen-stimulation-facial.html), [dermal-filler](../dermal-filler.html), [oligio-x](../oligio-x.html), [signature-treatment](../signature-treatment.html), etc.*)
* **`k-beauty` / `k-beauty-approach` / `seoul-inspired`**: Content linking the treatment to Korean beauty standards (*[about](../about.html), [tone-up-facial](../tone-up-facial.html), [oligio-x](../oligio-x.html)*)
* **`story` / `difference`**: Brand storytelling and unique differentiators (*[about](../about.html)*)
* **`transparency`**: Honest discussion about the treatment (*[tone-up-facial](../tone-up-facial.html)*)

## 7. Categorized Lists & Hubs
Sections used on "Hub" pages to list multiple child treatments.
* **`treatments` / `all-treatments` / `signature-treatments`**: Grids linking out to individual treatment pages (*[index](../index.html), [signature-treatment](../signature-treatment.html), [skin-health](../skin-health.html)*)
* **`injectables` / `biostimulators` / `ha-fillers` / `skin-boosters`**: Category-specific hubs (*[skin-health](../skin-health.html), [injectables](../injectables.html)*)
* **`medical-tox` / `cosmetic-tox` / `skintox`**: Toxin-specific category blocks (*[injectables](../injectables.html), [cosmetic-tox](../cosmetic-tox.html)*)

---

**Tip for Content Creators:**
When writing copy for a new treatment page, a standard flow usually consists of:
1. `hero` (Hook & Intro)
2. `what-is` (The Explanation)
3. `treatment-benefits` (The Value)
4. `expect` (The Experience)
5. `why-sua` (The Differentiator)
6. `faq` (The Reassurance)
