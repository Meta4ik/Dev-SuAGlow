HydroGlow FAQs

What is the Korean HydroGlow Facial?

The Korean HydroGlow Facial is an advanced multi-modality clinical treatment powered by the Eve Omega platform. It combines radial spiral vortex hydro-dermabrasion, 4-in-1 X-Stream chemical peeling, microcurrent ATP stimulation, lymphatic drainage, and EMS muscle contouring to deeply cleanse, hydrate, and re-sculpt the facial canvas.

How does HydroGlow differ from standard hydrafacials?

While basic hydrafacials stop at superficial suction and moisture, HydroGlow is a synchronized 90-minute medical ritual. It incorporates dual-frequency microcurrent to awaken cellular ATP energy and EMS (Electrical Muscle Stimulation) to lift slackened cheek and jaw muscles, delivering both deep hydration and structural V-line contouring.

What is the X-Stream solution?

X-Stream is Eve Omega's proprietary 4-in-1 clinical exfoliation complex formulated in Korea. It unites AHA (Lactic Acid), PHA (Lactobionic Acid), 4th-generation LHA (Capryloyl Salicylic Acid), and natural Bromelain enzymes to dissolve dead surface keratin bridges and sebum without scratching or stripping delicate barrier lipids.

Is there any downtime or redness?

Zero downtime. The treatment is gentle, calming, and soothing. Because it calms inflammation rather than inflicting micro-trauma, you can apply sunscreen or makeup and immediately resume your normal schedule, making it our most requested facial before weddings, galas, and photoshoots.

Can HydroGlow help with acne and clogged pores?

Yes. By combining radial vortex suction with LHA (a gentle, lipophilic acid with the same pH as healthy skin), HydroGlow effortlessly clears blackheads, excess sebum, and trapped impurities from clogged pores while reducing active breakout inflammation.

How often should I get a HydroGlow facial?

For maintenance and consistent Korean glass skin glow, most clients schedule a treatment every 3 to 4 weeks to sync with the skin's natural cellular turnover cycle. It is also available as a standalone pre-event prep facial 24 to 72 hours before a major occasion.
